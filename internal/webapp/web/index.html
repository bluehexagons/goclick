    Controls

    Press with a mouse or touch, then release on or near the button. Each finger counts separately. With the button focused, Space or Enter adds one click on release. Holding never adds extra clicks.

    Amber means the save is pending or the response is slow. Green and blue indicate fast and standard responses. Orange indicates a server cooldown. Red means the save failed or could not be confirmed.

    Game & storage notes (English)

    Clicks & saves

    Releasing on or near the button adds one click. The counter shows saved clicks, with pending clicks listed separately. Returning to this tab or reconnecting refreshes the counter.

    Streak periods

    Days, calendar months, and calendar years use UTC. Multiple clicks in the same period do not extend a streak. The next click after a missed period starts that streak at one.

    Ping & storage

    Online ping measures a click request, including server processing. +1 badges wait for confirmation and compare timing to the last 20 pings. Account progress stays in SQLite on the server. Server limits pause online saves briefly; a countdown shows when play resumes. Rejected clicks are not queued or replayed. Very regular or uninterrupted activity can also trigger a brief cooldown.

    Local play & sessions

    Offline play uses one browser-local save and simulated ping, with no account API calls. Options can download, import, restart, or copy account progress into that slot. Local clicks never transfer back to your account. Online activity keeps your session signed in; about 180 days of inactivity ends it.

    Local saves

    Clicks are saved in this browser. Use Options to download, import, or restart your save.
