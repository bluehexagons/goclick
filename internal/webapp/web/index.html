Game & storage notes

Clicks & saves

Releasing inside the button adds one click. Holding does not add extra clicks. The total shows server-confirmed saves; pending clicks appear separately. Returning to this tab or reconnecting refreshes saved progress. Failed clicks are never replayed.

Streak periods

Days, calendar months, and calendar years use UTC. Multiple clicks in the same period do not extend a streak. The next click after a missed period starts that streak at one.

Ping & storage

Online ping measures a click request, including server processing. +1 badges wait for confirmation and compare timing to the last 20 pings. Amber means pending or slow; red means failed or unconfirmed. Account progress stays in SQLite on the server.

Local play & sessions

Offline play uses one browser-local save and simulated ping, with no account API calls. Options can download, import, restart, or copy account progress into that slot. Local clicks never transfer back to your account. Online activity keeps your session signed in; about 180 days of inactivity ends it.
