# Go Click player guide

[Play Go Click on GitHub Pages](https://bluehexagons.github.io/goclick/) to
start clicking right away. Your progress is saved in your browser. On your
first connected visit, open **Options** and look under **Offline play**.
When you see **Ready to reopen offline**, you can close the tab and reopen
the game without a connection in the same browser. If offline reopening is
unavailable, keep the loaded tab open to play while disconnected.

## Controls and settings

- **Mouse, touch, or pen** — Press **Click** and release on or near the button.
  Each finger counts separately.
- **Keyboard** — Focus **Click**, then press and release Space or Enter.
- **Sound** — Effects start off. Use the speaker in the header to turn them on.
  In **Options**, choose a sound profile, adjust the volume or room reverb, and
  preview the sound without adding a click.
- **Motion** — Choose **Auto** to follow your device's setting, **Reduced** for
  quieter visuals, or **Full** for the full animation.
- **Language** — Choose from thirteen languages in the header. The game starts
  with your browser's preferred supported language. A surprise label on the
  button goes back to your chosen language on the next click.

Each release adds at most one click. Holding the button or releasing away from
it does not add clicks. The main counter shows saved clicks. Pending clicks
appear separately until they are confirmed.

## Saves and accounts

On GitHub Pages, the game always uses your local save. Choose **Play online**
in **Options** to open the [online edition](https://clicker.bluehexagons.com/).
Sign in there to keep account progress across devices.

On a Go Click server, press **Click** while signed out or choose **Play
offline** in **Options** to start a local game. Use **Back online** in the
header to return to the server. Local play on a Go server needs the page to
stay loaded while you're disconnected.

Your local save and account progress are separate. Switching back online keeps
your local save in the browser. Local clicks never upload to your account.

### Move or replace a local save

1. Open **Options** and select **Download save** on the site where you played.
2. Open the game on the destination site or browser and go to **Options**.
3. Under **Replace local save**, check **Allow replacing my local save**.
4. Select **Import save** and choose the downloaded JSON file.

Import replaces the destination save. Use **Download save** before clearing
site data, moving to a different browser or device, or replacing a save you
want to keep. **Restart save** clears the local slot after the same
confirmation. On a Go Click server, **Copy account progress** can fill the
local slot with a snapshot of your account after you open its counter.

## Streaks and achievements

One saved click keeps a streak going for the current period. Days, weeks,
months, and years use UTC, with weeks starting Monday. Hover over or activate
the ring beside the day streak to see when the next day starts in your time
zone.

Open **Achievements** to see your activity and filter badges by **Earned** or
**Locked**. Badges track milestones such as total clicks, streaks, and clicks
at different hours of the day. Earned badges survive breaks. Choose
**Reveal locked requirements** if you want exact targets alongside the hints.

**Fortnight activity** scores the 14 UTC days ending on your most recent
saved click. Each active day earns 5 points and each adjacent pair adds 2
points. The game scales the result to 100. A click on a new UTC day moves
the window forward.

## Share your progress

Select **Share** to copy a plain-text summary with your click total, streaks,
and a link to the game. The dialog lets you choose which details to include
and copy the updated text. If your browser blocks clipboard access, you can
copy the preview manually.
